# Privacy Policy – ANIMAcommonsouls Marketing App

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This app is a private tool used only by the owner of the ANIMAcommonsouls Etsy shop
(https://www.etsy.com/shop/ANIMAcommonsouls) to plan and publish marketing content for the shop's own products.

## What data the app accesses
- The shop owner's own Pinterest account data: boards, Pins and basic analytics.
- Public Pinterest trend data (popular search keywords) used to choose titles and tags for the shop's own listings.

## What the app does not do
- It does not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
- It does not run ads or track users.

## Storage and security
Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private CI environment and are never published.
Trend keywords may be stored in the app's configuration to improve listing text. No personal data is stored.

## Contact
Questions: through the ANIMAcommonsouls Etsy shop contact page.
